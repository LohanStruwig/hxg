# HXG findings — release 0.2.0

## Thesis

The in-room display is becoming an experience-orchestration layer connecting guest comfort, property operations, and ecosystem value.

This is a supported inference, not a universal causal claim. It is grounded in substantial independent guest use of smart-TV capabilities and in current cross-vendor architectures that connect casting, cloud management, property systems, IoT controls, services, analytics, privacy controls, and content.

## Strongest direct evidence

- J.D. Power reports smart-TV availability in 74% of surveyed branded hotel rooms and 62% guest usage in its 2026 North America study, based on 44,787 branded hotel guests (`CLM-JDP-AVAIL-01`, `CLM-JDP-USAGE-01`, `CLM-JDP-SAMPLE-01`).
- AHLA projects nearly $805B in 2026 U.S. hotel guest spending while operating expenses keep GOPPAR at roughly 90% of 2019 levels (`CLM-AHLA-SPEND-01`, `CLM-AHLA-GOPPAR-01`). These are industry context, not screen-attributable value.
- ENERGY STAR lodging guidance places energy at about 6% of operating costs and heating/cooling at almost 40% of electricity use (`CLM-ENERGY-COST-01`, `CLM-ENERGY-HVAC-01`). These are current guidance figures, not newly measured 2026 data.
- Current Samsung, LG, Apple, Google, and Oracle records establish that hospitality displays can participate in casting, cloud management, service, PMS, data, and integration pathways. Product outcome and financial claims remain unproven without independent or property evidence.

## Human-outcome map

HXG organizes the experience into six interpretable outcomes: feel at home, in control, recognized, included, secure, and supported. This is a research ontology used to connect evidence—not a new psychometric scale.

## Value model

Human value is kept in human terms unless evidence supports conversion. Property savings and ancillary contribution are modeled as transparent scenarios using property-specific inputs. Ecosystem mechanisms are mapped without estimating private vendor revenue. Macroeconomic figures are labeled context only.

## Principal contradictions

1. Availability and usage do not establish causal satisfaction or revenue.
2. An announced feature is not universally available.
3. Current guidance can contain older benchmark context.
4. Industry spending is not attributable to a screen.
5. Autonomous research still has human governance.
6. Translation is not complete accessibility.

The complete evidence chain is in `data/public/` and the explorer.
